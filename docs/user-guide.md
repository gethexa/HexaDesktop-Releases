## 1. Overview

HexaDesktop is the desktop application for the Hexa platform. It gives you everything available in the Hexa web app, plus features that only a native app can offer:

- Starts with your computer and runs in the system tray / menu bar
- Native notifications
- Keyboard shortcuts for every action
- Works offline and syncs when you reconnect
- Automatic updates

## 2. Your account

### Signing in and out

Sign in with your Hexa account on first launch. To sign out, open the **Account menu** (top-right) → **Sign out**.

### Switching accounts

Open **Account menu → Switch account** to sign in with a different Hexa account without losing your local settings.

### Managing your profile

**Account menu → Profile** opens your profile, where you can change your name, avatar and password.

## 3. Navigating the app

- Use the **sidebar** to move between sections. Collapse it with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd>).
- Go **back** and **forward** with <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>→</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>[</kbd> / <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>]</kbd>).
- Open multiple **windows** with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd>).

## 4. Working with your content

- **Create** new items with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd>) or the **+ New** button.
- **Drag and drop** files from your computer straight into the app to upload them.
- **Right-click** any item for its context menu (rename, duplicate, share, delete…).
- Deleted items go to the **Trash**, where they can be restored for 30 days.

## 5. Search

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>) to open the command palette. From there you can:

- Find any item by name
- Jump to any section of the app
- Run any command by typing its name

## 6. Sync and offline mode

HexaDesktop keeps your data in sync with your Hexa account automatically. The **status bar** shows the current state:

| Indicator | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 🟢 **Synced** | Everything is up to date |
| 🔄 **Syncing** | Changes are being uploaded or downloaded |
| 🟠 **Offline** | No connection — your changes are saved locally and will sync later |
| 🔴 **Sync error** | Something went wrong — click the indicator for details |

## 7. Notifications

HexaDesktop uses your operating system's native notifications. Choose what you are notified about in **Settings → Notifications**. Use **Do not disturb** to pause all notifications temporarily.

## 8. Appearance and language

In **Settings → Appearance** you can choose:

- **Theme:** Light, Dark or System
- **Interface zoom:** <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>+</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>-</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>0</kbd>
- **Language:** under **Settings → Language**

## 9. Privacy and data

- Your data is transferred over encrypted connections (TLS).
- Local data is stored in your user profile folder (see [Uninstalling](installation.md#uninstalling) for its location).
- You can opt out of anonymous usage statistics in **Settings → Privacy**.

## 10. Getting help from inside the app

- **Help → Documentation** opens these docs.
- **Help → Report a problem** collects logs and opens a pre-filled bug report.
- **Help → About HexaDesktop** shows your version number — include it in every support request.
