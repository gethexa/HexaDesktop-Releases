# Getting started

Welcome to HexaDesktop! This guide walks you through your first few minutes with the app.

## 1. Install HexaDesktop

If you haven't already, follow the [installation guide](installation.md).

## 2. Sign in

When you open HexaDesktop for the first time you'll see the welcome screen.

1. Click **Sign in**.
2. Enter the email address and password of your Hexa account — the same account you use on the Hexa web app.
3. If two-factor authentication is enabled, enter the code from your authenticator app.

Don't have an account yet? Click **Create account** on the welcome screen.

> [!TIP]
> HexaDesktop remembers your session. You'll stay signed in until you choose **Account → Sign out**.

## 3. Take a tour of the interface

| Area | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Sidebar** (left) | Navigate between the main sections of the app |
| **Main area** (center) | Where you view and work on your content |
| **Toolbar** (top) | Quick actions, search and notifications |
| **Status bar** (bottom) | Connection and sync status |
| **Account menu** (top-right) | Profile, settings and sign out |

## 4. Your first steps

- **Search anything** with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on macOS).
- **Open settings** with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>,</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>,</kbd> on macOS) to choose your theme, language and notifications.
- **Stay in sync** — everything you do in HexaDesktop is synced with your Hexa account and available on the web.
